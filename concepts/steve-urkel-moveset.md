# Steve Urkel — invention/comedy fighter

Player 2's first playable opponent for Will. Other Will transformations are on
hold at the user's request. Keep Urkel's own face, large round glasses, red
suspenders, yellow collared shirt, high-waisted indigo trousers and saddle shoes.

Urkel fights through awkward accidents and overconfident inventions. Light palm,
elbow bump, long-legged kick, low poke/sweep and aerial kick form his normals.
Movement is deliberately more awkward and slightly slower than Will's.

- Cheese Please: a small cheese projectile, quarter-circle forward + light.
- Did I Do That?: forward stumble/body bump, quarter-circle back + medium or skill.
- Suspender Snap: close overhead palm, quarter-circle forward + heavy.
- Safety First: vulnerable backward retreat, quarter-circle back + heavy.
- Super, Gadget Malfunction (1000 meter): the homemade gadget sputters, kicks
  backward and finally erupts with an oversized unstable blast.
- Ultimate, Urkelbot Unleashed (2000 meter): close hit confirms into a huge robot
  summon. The robot barrels in, juggles the opponent with mechanical punches,
  then delivers a spectacular uppercut/slam. Urkel adjusts his glasses innocently.
  No cheese-overload finisher, taxi or Agent J orbital sequence.
- Stefan Urquelle transformation: the same character becomes confident and
  smooth, with his own polished outfit and close-combat kit. Shared health and
  remaining meter, vulnerable switch, free return to Steve. This transformation
  is part of Urkel's scope; other Will transformations remain on hold.

First pass uses original transparent key poses with some shared drawings.
Health 1000, maximum meter 3000, standard guarding,
throws and knockdown recovery. Both characters keep their own costumes when
captured by an opponent's throws or cinematic moves.
