# Sung Jinwoo — Shadow Monarch

User selected Shadow Monarch: twin daggers, shadow summons and a shadow-army
ultimate. Add him as a separate roster fighter; retain Will and Steve/Stefan.

Lean dark-haired anime silhouette, black long coat over fitted black clothes,
violet eyes and twin dark daggers with violet edges. Preserve the game's crisp
cel-shaded sprite presentation while keeping Jinwoo's anime identity.

Fast standing, low and air dagger attacks; a knockdown kick; forward/back throws.
Shadow Step is a short vulnerable forward approach, not a free invincible escape.
Quarter-circle forward + light throws a dagger. Shadows are directly selected:
skill calls Igris, toward + skill calls Beru, away + skill calls Iron, and
holding down while pressing skill calls Tank. Each has a distinct fixed input.
Quarter-circle back + light calls Igris, quarter-circle back + medium calls Beru,
back-back + skill calls Iron, and down-down + skill calls Tank.
Quarter-circle forward + heavy is an overhead lunging slash. Quarter-circle back
+ heavy retreats with no invulnerability.

Super: Dagger Tempest: Monarch Storm, 1000 meter, 620 damage, four seconds after
confirmation. Teleport pursuit, Beru assists, dagger rain and a final pillar.
Ultimate: Monarch's Decree: Arise, 2000 meter, 950 damage, seven seconds after
confirmation. A guardable screen-wide starter opens the domain; Tank charges,
Iron launches, Igris cuts, Beru pursues, then the army converges for a three-pillar
execution. Displayed fresh counters are 43 hits for super and 85 for ultimate.
Summons stay framed in screen space so corner fights cannot clip the army.

User direction: make finishers intentionally overpowered and exaggerated.
Current shadow damage: Igris 100, Beru 110, Iron 125, Tank 115. Each costs 250
meter and shares a 90-tick cooldown. Existing eight-frame walk/jump/dagger cycles
remain; some kick, crouch and reaction drawings still share poses.

No transformation in this first kit. Health 1000, maximum meter 3000. First pass
uses original transparent key poses with shared walk/attack drawings; portraits,
voices, full animation in-betweens and balance remain unfinished.
