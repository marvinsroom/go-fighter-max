Controls and movesets

Each form has 17 attacks: nine standing/crouching/aerial normals, four signatures, three supers and its own ultimate. Controls follow the same pattern as Jinwoo: Z/X/C attacks, A signatures, S supers, D ultimate. Tap and release A/S/D. Directions are relative to the opponent.
