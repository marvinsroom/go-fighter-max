Original Baam and Jue Viole Grace

Start as short-haired Baam in his red waistcoat, charcoal scarf and tan trousers. W plays eight connected stages into long-haired Viole: his hair lengthens and the black FUG robe forms, including its red and white ring panel. Both appearances keep the same body height. W reverses the sequence; health and meter are shared.

Viole persists through attacks, movement and damage. The transition can be interrupted; getting hit before the change commits preserves the previous appearance. Endless Sky opens a constellation of baang, suspends the opponent in a gravity well, raises a Shinsu pillar and ends with an enormous discharge. Original Baam releases blue-white Shinsu for 600 total damage; Viole amplifies the finish with crimson-black Thorn energy for 680. Blocked or missed openers recover without pulling the opponent into the sequence.

Character and artwork

Baam is from Tower of God by SIU. This custom fan adaptation uses original Baam and Jue Viole Grace designs, with authored Shinsu gameplay. W provides a stylized switch between these eras. Original anime character references guided the likeness, hair and costumes.

Impact and flow

Dedicated technique poses give the palm a weight transfer, Flow Piercer a low dash and afterimages, baang a visible aimed launch, Floral Butterfly a palm/elbow/rising-palm sequence, and Thorn Wave an overhead cut. Counters stop and return the strike. Hit sparks, short impact pauses and layered sound emphasize contact. Confirmed standing normals can cancel into a technique or ultimate.
