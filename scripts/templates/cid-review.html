Proportions & movement

Inspect all 56 Cid poses at a shared world scale. Crouches and lunges retain their natural shape.

Cid uses fixed scales through walking, airborne movement and combat, with upright cinematic drawings matched to his idle height. Connected-figure registration prevents stray crop pixels from moving his feet. Forward and backward walks use the same drawings in reverse. Atomic body copies share Cid's normal scale, and the shared camera stays stable.
