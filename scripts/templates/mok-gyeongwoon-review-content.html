Controls

Arrows move, crouch and jump. Hold back to guard; double-tap forward or back to dash. Tap combo follow-ups as a strike connects. Missed starters cannot chain. A confirmed standing normal can cancel into a skill or ultimate.

Mayhem of the Heavenly Demon

The 3000-meter opener is guardable. Only a confirmed hit starts the 164-tick staged finisher, for 660 total damage. Both fighters stay in front of the effects, and the attack makes room near the facing edge to keep the victim visible. Blocks and misses recover without forced capture.
