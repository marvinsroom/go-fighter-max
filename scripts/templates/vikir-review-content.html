Controls

Arrows move, crouch and jump. Hold away from the opponent to guard. Press attack keys to activate moves. Hold a direction before pressing for a directional skill.

Guests share a six-second cooldown. Calls can be interrupted; healing caps at maximum life. Guest attacks and Anubis arrows travel across the arena and can be guarded.

Manhwa appearances

Hunter Vikir, the masked Night Hound disguise, Choco, and the Colosseo academy uniform with glasses. Choco has a small hurtbox; sword attacks restore the human body. Night Hound shares Vikir’s sword moves. Sword Aura represents mastery as a timed game power-up.
