Controls

Arrows move, crouch and jump. Hold back to guard; double-tap forward or back to dash. Tap combo follow-ups as a strike connects. Missed starters cannot chain. A confirmed standing normal can cancel into a skill or ultimate.

Demonic Emperor Ascendant

The 3000-meter opener is guardable. Only a confirmed hit starts the 164-tick staged finisher, for 680 total damage. Both fighters stay in front of the effects, and the attack makes room near the facing edge to keep the victim visible. Blocks and misses recover without forced capture.

Character and artwork

Barehanded demonic palm strikes, azure flame, lightning, devouring mist and a Demonic Emperor Ascendant cinematic. This is custom fan gameplay based on Demonic Emperor / The Servant Is the Demon King, credited to Ye Xiao / HongShu / WUER Comic / Kuaikan. No third-party MUGEN release is claimed.
