Light, medium and heavy attacks chain on contact; a connected normal can cancel into a skill or super. Misses retain recovery. The quiet ultimate uses one unseen pass, a still pause, subtle silk and a sudden finishing cut.

Fine silk and quiet assassination

Thread and ultimate close-up review

The current artwork follows the likeness study accepted from the supplied manhwa reference: high-tied long white hair, red slit eyes, a black robe over dark crossed lapels, and a white snake on his shoulder. Combat centers on traps, hidden weapons and precise silk cuts. Snare holds an opponent in place to set up a follow-up. The ultimate replaces the red disk and web with a quiet ink-and-mist scene. Fine silk tightens from the casting hand before the final cut.

The current animation pass uses connected wind-up, contact, follow-through and recovery drawings, with one shared scale per source sheet. The threads and moon are separate arena effects.
