Controls

Arrows move, crouch and jump. Hold back to guard; double-tap forward or back to dash. Tap combo follow-ups as a strike connects. Missed starters cannot chain. A confirmed standing normal can cancel into a skill or ultimate.

Heaven and Earth Collapse

The 3000-meter opener is guardable. Only a confirmed hit starts the 164-tick staged finisher, for 620 total damage. Both fighters stay in front of the effects, and the attack makes room near the facing edge to keep the victim visible. Blocks and misses recover without forced capture.

Character and artwork

Lean manhwa Gang Yong: barehanded palm combinations, internal-force pressure, stone-splitting shockwaves and Heaven and Earth Collapse. This is custom fan gameplay based on GOSU, credited to Giun Ryu / Mun Jeong Hoo. No third-party MUGEN release is claimed. One illustrated appearance; W is Dumpling Break.
