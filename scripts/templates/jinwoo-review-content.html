Supers begin with a brief eight-tick power pause, then stay in normal combat. Their finishing hits knock opponents down. They can be blocked or miss and only need meter. Longer capture sequences belong to ultimates. Ultimates also need the separate Ruler gauge: keep charging even when the main meter is full. Switching weapons clears Domain.
