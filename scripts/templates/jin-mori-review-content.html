Controls

Arrows move, crouch and jump; hold away to guard. Double-tap a direction to dash. Hold Enter to charge power.

For combos, tap each next key while the previous strike connects. A short input buffer accepts slightly early presses. Chains also work on guard; a missed starter cannot cancel into its follow-up. Hold the direction before pressing a skill shortcut.

The staff ultimate begins with a guardable sweep. Only a confirmed hit enters the cinematic. Both fighters remain visible; wind trails sit behind their bodies. Misses and blocks recover without the cinematic.

Two persistent appearances

BASE / RENEWAL TAEKWONDO

Jin Mori

A new round entrance adjusts his goggles and greets his opponent. Buffered punch and kick chains lead into his counter and dragon supers.

W / TRANSFORMATION

Monkey King

Eight transformation drawings show his goggles becoming the circlet, wraps appearing and tiger pelt forming before he settles into guard. Both forms retain the same planted height. The transformation takes about 1.5 seconds; W reverses it. The appearance persists through movement, attacks and damage. Health and power are shared; awakening can be interrupted.

The transformation, combo buffering and staged staff finish are authored game adaptations of the imported fighter. The original source is preserved separately. Key poses still share some in-betweens; functional validation does not establish visual approval.
