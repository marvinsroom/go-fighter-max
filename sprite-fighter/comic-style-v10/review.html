Naruto and Saitama: illustrated conversion

Installed illustrated artwork. 72 of 72 sheets are saved. All planned sheets and both portraits passed layout checks and are installed in the playable characters. Full Kurama is now 1.5 times larger, with matching collision boxes; that approved scale carries into the conversion. Recordings remain deferred.

Eight Naruto form banks are covered by the plan: base, Sage, Ashura, KCM1, KCM2, four tails, six tails and Kurama. Saitama and his existing assists are included. Saved progress and remaining work · Lloyd and Mori redesigns
