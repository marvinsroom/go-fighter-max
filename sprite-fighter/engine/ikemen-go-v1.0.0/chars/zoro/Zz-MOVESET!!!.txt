Lista de Movimientos.
  
- Taunt                  = Start

** Normals:

- Combo 1                = a
- Combo 2                = b

- Grab                   = a or b        (close to the opponent)

** Specials:

- Stand Special          = c
- Side Special           = F,c
- Down Special           = D,c           (Counter)

- Air Atacks             = a,b or c
- Air Special            = D,c

** Supers:

- Rokudou No Tsuji       = D,DF,F,a      Power = 1000
- Kokuhyou Ootatsumaki   = D,DF,F,b      Power = 2000
- San Zen Sekai          = D,DF,F,c      Power = 3000

** TEAM MODE:

- Change                 = a+b           Power = 500
- Call Helper            = b+c           Power = 500
- Hyper Dual             = D,DF,F,a+b    Power = 3000  

** Counter Ability:

- TatsuMaki              = B,c           Power = 1500 (when you get hurt)
