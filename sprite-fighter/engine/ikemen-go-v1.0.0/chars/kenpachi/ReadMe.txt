;======================================================
basics are obvious and easy to pick up from a quick play (however slightly differ
for combos and depending on the state).
Parry - press forward when being attacked
Specials:
	D,B,x/y/z - sword super. keep pressing for a 3 hit combo
	D,b,a/b/c - sword super (diff). keep pressing for a 3 hit combo.
	D,F,a/b/c = upper slice
	D,F,x/y/z = forward slice (wall bounce)
		-it varies depending on combo.
supers:
	D,F,a+b = teleportation super (1 super bar)
	D,F,x+y = slice super         (1 super bar)
hyper:
	D,B,a+b = Hyper move! (similar to SGS) (3 super bars).
general chainability and kenpachi style attacks.
