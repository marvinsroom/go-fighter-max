# Illustrated JUS movement

The shared movement layer applies to all 23 playable manga/manhwa fighters. Use the existing Play or Fight CPU launchers. Character attacks and form controls remain in each fighter's review.

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Q while airborne | Air dash, once per jump. |
| Back + Q while airborne | Retreating air dash. |
| Q after hitting an airborne opponent | Dash toward that hit target. |
| Hold Up when a grounded normal connects | Launch the opponent for 100 meter. |
| Connect an air normal, then another attack | Up to two additional confirmed air cancels per jump. |
| Up + Q while airborne | Toggle sustained flight for Goku, GT Goku, Ichigo, Rimuru and Jin Mori. |

Flight requires 300 meter to enter and spends 5 meter per tick. Directions steer; a hit interrupts flight. Release Q between toggles. Ichigo's ordinary Shunpo remains available; airborne Up+Q takes priority for flight. W retains native form controls.

Landing restores the air dash and cancel allowance. Air dashes have hurt boxes and vulnerable recovery. Launchers require a real normal hit and do not trigger from whiffs or blocks.

Training Room slabs crack and become rubble when nearby fighters take actual damage. They are visual scenery, so they cannot absorb an attack or become a combo target. Landings raise dust, hard knockdowns leave bounded floor scars, and corner hits shake the arena. The training camera follows aerial separation and returns to ground framing.

Current illustrated artwork and earlier relative proportions are retained at compact size. Naruto and Saitama retain their imported pixel art while new-character art is on hold. Saitama retains his source's damage immunity.
