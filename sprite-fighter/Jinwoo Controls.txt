JINWOO - XBOX CONTROLLER

Left stick    Move, crouch, and jump
A             Light attack
B             Medium attack
X             Grab / throw (at close range)
Y             Heavy attack
RB            Ultimate — Mutilate / King Domain (meter + special bar)
LB (hold)     Charge meter and special bar
LT            Summon shadow
RT            Super attack (requires 1 meter bar)
Start         Pause
Back          Menu
D-pad Up      Igris transformation
D-pad Right   Beru transformation
D-pad Left    Iron transformation
D-pad Down    Tank transformation
Right stick down  Change sword / dagger set
Right stick up    Mutilate / King Domain (meter + special bar)
Right stick left  Monarch Domain
Right stick right Antares / Spoiler Skill (meter + special bar)
Right stick click Skill 11 (1 bar)
Left stick click  Skill 12 (1 bar)
Hold away       Guard standing and overhead attacks
Hold down-away  Guard low attacks

Additional human-form moves:
Quarter-circle forward + A / B / RT   Skills 1 / 3 / 5
Quarter-circle back + A / B / RT      Skills 2 / 4 / 6
Down + A / B / RT                     Skills 7 / 8 / 9

Summon selection while human:
