# Character modeling reset: strictly free

User explicitly requires a strictly free solution. No subscriptions, paid exports,
paid inference, or paid artists are authorized. Unreal remains the game engine.

## Finding

The integrated Will, TRELLIS preview and Pixal3D preview all failed the user's
likeness/style review. Pixal3D generation completing is a technical success only.
Its stretched head, changed physique and clothing silhouette are not an acceptable
character. Stop advancing these candidates into rigging and Unreal integration.

## Recommended free workflow

Use the already installed Blender for direct character modeling/sculpting and
material work. It provides the necessary tools, but those tools do not supply
expert artistic judgment automatically. There is no verified free one-click
generator in our tests that delivers the requested Will likeness and Guilty Gear
style. Do not promise another generator will solve it.

Work on a head-only study first: establish front/profile facial proportions,
recognizable ears, mouth, cheek/jaw planes and hair silhouette. Review actual
geometry with neutral/clay shading and a separate toon-shaded view. Add body,
costume, rigging and game integration after the head likeness is accepted.
Photographic texture projection must not substitute for matching geometry.
Do not resume the previously rejected landmark-fitting method.

VRoid Studio is a genuinely free anime avatar editor with VRM export. It is an
alternative for generic anime avatars, not evidence that Will's specific likeness
can be reproduced faithfully. Installing it is not necessary for this reset and
it has not been installed.
